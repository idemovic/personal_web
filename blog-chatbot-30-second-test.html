The 30-Second Test That Reveals a Bad AI Chatbot

Published on 2026-08-02

One question will reveal the quality of an AI chatbot in 30 seconds.

In a demo, ask something that is definitely not answered in the documentation.

A quality chatbot will say:

"I don't have this information, I'll connect you with a colleague."

A poor one will invent an answer. Smoothly, confidently and incorrectly.

Why It Matters

This is called hallucination, and in customer support it is the difference between a tool you trust and a tool that will one day invent a complaint policy for you that you don't have.

What a Serious Solution Does

A serious solution will limit AI to your company's knowledge base: what is not in the documentation, the AI will not answer, it will hand it over to a human. Test this behavior with every tool you are considering. Before signing, not after.
